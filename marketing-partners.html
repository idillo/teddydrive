Marketing Partners

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Summary: TeddyDrive.com is an independent consumer-marketing website operated by Idillo Inc. When you submit an insurance request and provide the consent shown on our form, we may share the information you provide with one or more of the recipients described below so they can respond to your request.

Who We Are

TeddyDrive.com is a marketing website. Idillo Inc. is not an insurance carrier, agency, broker, or producer and does not underwrite, bind, issue, or guarantee insurance policies.

Categories of Recipients

When you submit an insurance request, we may share the contact and quote-related information you provide with one or more of the following categories of recipients so they can provide insurance information or follow up on your request:

  • Licensed insurance carriers authorized to issue auto insurance in your state.
  • Licensed insurance agencies, agents, and brokers appointed by one or more carriers.
  • Managing general agents authorized to represent insurance carriers.
  • Marketing partners that operate consumer-facing insurance comparison or matching services.
  • Service providers that support hosting, analytics, fraud prevention, identity verification, and consent management under agreements restricting their use of the information.

Telephone and Email Contact

The recipients described on this page may use the telephone number and email address you provide to contact you about the insurance request you submitted, subject to the consent shown on the request form.

Service Providers

We may use service providers to operate the website, attribute conversions, prevent fraud, verify information, and record consent. These providers receive information only as needed to perform those services and are not authorized by us to use your full insurance request for their own marketing.

Communication Choices

You may ask TeddyDrive or a recipient that contacts you to stop future telephone calls or email. A request made to TeddyDrive does not automatically stop communications from a recipient with whom your information has already been shared; contact that recipient directly as well.

Request a Recipient List

You may request information about the recipients with whom your insurance-request information was shared. Requests may be directed to Idillo Inc. using the contact information published on TeddyDrive.com. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

Changes

Our recipient network may change periodically. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page reflects the most recent material revision to this disclosure.

Contact

Questions about this disclosure may be directed to Idillo Inc. through the contact information published on TeddyDrive.com.
