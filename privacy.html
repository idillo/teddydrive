Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Idillo Inc. (“Idillo,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), the operator of TeddyDrive.com, collects, uses, and discloses information when you visit this website or call a telephone number displayed on it.

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you provide during a telephone call, such as your name, contact details, state, vehicle information, driving history, current insurance information, and the insurance coverage you are seeking. Do not provide sensitive information unless it is necessary for the insurance inquiry.

We may also automatically receive technical and usage information, including your IP address, browser and device information, referring page, pages viewed, approximate location derived from your IP address, and interactions with advertisements or call buttons.

Telephone Calls

Telephone calls may be routed through a call-tracking platform and may be monitored or recorded for quality assurance, training, fraud prevention, compliance, and service-improvement purposes where permitted by law. When a recording disclosure is required, it may be provided at the beginning of the call.

How We Use Information

  • To respond to your request and connect you with a participating licensed insurance agency, agent, or provider.
  • To operate, analyze, protect, and improve TeddyDrive.com and its advertising campaigns.
  • To measure calls and advertising performance.
  • To detect misuse, fraud, security incidents, or unlawful activity.
  • To comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

How Information May Be Shared

Information may be shared with participating licensed insurance agencies, agents, carriers, call-routing providers, analytics and advertising providers, hosting providers, professional advisers, and other vendors that help us operate the website and process your request. Insurance agencies and providers receiving your information may process it under their own privacy policies.

Cookies, Analytics, and Advertising

We may use cookies, pixels, local storage, call-tracking technology, Google Ads, Google Analytics, or similar services to understand website use, attribute calls, prevent fraud, and measure advertising performance. You can restrict cookies through your browser settings, although some website functions may not operate correctly.

Your Privacy Choices

You may request access to or deletion of personal information that we maintain about you, subject to applicable law and verification. You may also ask us not to sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under applicable state privacy laws. Because this website is designed primarily to generate an insurance call, the fastest way to avoid providing information is not to call or to end the call before providing personal details.

Data Retention and Security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, including legal, compliance, fraud-prevention, and business-record requirements. We use reasonable safeguards, but no transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children

TeddyDrive.com is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date above indicates when it was last revised.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests may be directed to Idillo Inc. through the contact information published for the company or through the telephone number displayed on TeddyDrive.com.
